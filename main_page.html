What we do

Three quiet pillars of elder support.

From the moment a senior reaches out, we tailor care across financial relief, residential placement, and a partner network, so no one is left without help.

01

Financial assistance

Direct relief for medical bills, daily essentials, and recurring costs, given confidentially and without bureaucracy, to seniors facing hardship.

02

Elderly home placement

Who we are

A quiet promise, kept for twenty-five years.

Amitabha Foundation Elder Home opened on Old Klang Road in 2000 with one simple commitment: that every elder we welcome lives the rest of their life in dignity. That promise has not changed.

Compassion as practice

Care isn't a service we sell; it's a discipline our team renews every shift.

Dignity by design

From private moments to public meals, residents are treated as family members, not patients.

Transparent giving

Every donation is tax-deductible and traceable to the care it funds.

Life at the Home

Where ordinary days become quietly extraordinary

Morning chai on the verandah, afternoon games in the day room, garden walks before dinner. A glimpse of the moments that make Amitabha feel like home.

Community life

Togetherness every day

Residents gather in the shaded courtyard for morning tea, afternoon chats, and the slow pace that makes this home feel like family.

Resident portrait

Every face tells a story

Behind every smile is a life fully lived. Our residents bring decades of wisdom, warmth, and resilience to our home every single day.

Health care

Wellness checked, every day

Our staff conduct regular health screenings: blood pressure, oxygen, blood glucose, so every resident stays monitored and cared for.

Community visits

Bridging generations

School groups and youth volunteers visit regularly, bringing energy, laughter, and a reminder that the wider community cares for its elders.

Spiritual wellbeing

A space for peace & reflection

Our prayer room offers residents a quiet sanctuary for daily devotion. Spiritual care is part of whole-person wellbeing at Amitabha.

Why families choose us

Medical access
