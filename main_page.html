Who we are

A quiet promise, kept for twenty-five years.

Amitabha Foundation Elder Home opened on Old Klang Road in 2000 with one simple commitment: that every elder we welcome lives the rest of their life in dignity. That promise has not changed.

Compassion as practice

Dignity by design

Common Questions

Answers for families.

A few things families ask us most often. For the full list, visit our FAQ page.

How much does it cost for a resident to stay?
Monthly fees range from RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 depending on the resident's level of care. Many residents receive subsidies — some stay for free. No one is turned away due to financial hardship.
What types of elderly can be accommodated?
We welcome elderly who are mobile, wheelchair-bound, or bedridden, including those with dementia or special needs. Care is tailored to each resident's condition.
Do residents need to be Buddhist to stay here?
Not at all. All races and religions are welcome. Residents are free to practise their own beliefs. Our care is open to everyone equally.
What are the visitation hours?
Families are welcome to visit daily from 9am – 5pm. Our office operates Monday to Friday, 9am – 5pm.
How do I apply for my family member to stay?
Complete an application form and arrange a basic medical check-up to confirm the absence of communicable illnesses. Required documents: IC, completed form, and latest medical results.
Is there tax relief for donations?
Yes. The Amitabha Foundation is registered under LHDN, so qualifying monetary donations are eligible for tax exemption. Please retain your receipt for tax filing.
