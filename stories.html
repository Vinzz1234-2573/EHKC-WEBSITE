Featured Story

A life reclaimed

Mr. Kumar, 84
Petaling Jaya · Resident since 2022
"
Resident Story

Diabetes left me unable to walk. Three months of physiotherapy and proper meals gave me back my independence.

When Mr. Kumar first arrived at Amitabha Foundation Elder Home, he could barely stand on his own. Complications from long-term diabetes had weakened his legs and dimmed his confidence. Within three months of daily physiotherapy, four nutritious meals, and round-the-clock nursing support, he was walking the garden path on his own every morning. "They did not just treat my legs," he told our team. "They gave me back my belief that I could still live fully." Today, Mr. Kumar leads a morning stretching group for other residents and makes sure no one misses breakfast.
